# SOM-Service-Operations-Project

## SERVQUAL Service Quality Study of Triveni Terrace Café

### Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Service Operations Management (SOM)** course. The study evaluates service quality at **Triveni Terrace Café** using the SERVQUAL framework.

The analysis compares customer expectations and perceptions to identify service-quality gaps and develop practical improvement recommendations.

---

## Project Objective

The main objectives of the study are:

- To understand customer expectations regarding service quality.
- To evaluate customers' perceptions of the service delivered.
- To identify gaps between expectations and perceptions.
- To understand the key areas affecting customer satisfaction.
- To recommend practical improvements for better service delivery.

---

## Methodology

The project uses the **SERVQUAL model**, which evaluates service quality across five dimensions:

### 1. Tangibles

Evaluates the physical appearance of the café, facilities, equipment, employees and other visible elements of the service environment.

### 2. Reliability

Evaluates the ability of the café to provide the promised service accurately and consistently.

### 3. Responsiveness

Evaluates employees' willingness and ability to provide prompt service and respond to customer needs.

### 4. Assurance

Evaluates employees' knowledge, professionalism, courtesy and ability to create customer confidence and trust.

### 5. Empathy

Evaluates the level of individual attention, care and understanding provided to customers.

---

## SERVQUAL Analysis

The study uses customer responses to compare:

**Customer Expectations → Customer Perceptions → Service Gap**

A service gap occurs when the customer's perception of the delivered service differs from the expected level of service.

The analysis helps identify dimensions where improvement can create a better customer experience.

---

## Key Areas of Analysis

The project examines:

- Physical service environment
- Service reliability
- Employee responsiveness
- Employee professionalism and assurance
- Customer attention and empathy
- Overall service-quality gaps
- Opportunities for operational improvement

---

## Recommendations

Based on the identified service-quality gaps, improvement actions can focus on:

1. Maintaining consistent service standards.
2. Improving employee responsiveness to customer requests.
3. Strengthening employee training and service communication.
4. Maintaining a clean and attractive service environment.
5. Providing more personalized attention to customers.
6. Regularly collecting customer feedback to monitor service quality.
